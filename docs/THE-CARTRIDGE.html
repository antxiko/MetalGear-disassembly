The cartridge

The machine

MSX2: the VDP is a V9938 and the game works in SCREEN 5 (256 × 212, 16 colours, CHGMOD at p01:7C6C). VRAM page 0 is the screen and page 1 holds the tiles, the item drawings, the font and the backgrounds that get covered; almost everything is painted by copying rectangles from page 1 to page 0 with the VDP commands (p00:4FCD HMMM, p00:5011 LMMM with TIMP, p00:4F91 HMMV).

The mapper

A 128 KB MegaROM with the Konami mapper without SCC: 16 banks of 8 KB. Bank 0 is fixed at 0x4000; the other three are chosen by writing to 0x6000, 0x8000 and 0xA000. Bank 3k+1 goes to 0x6000, 3k+2 to 0x8000 and 3k+3 to 0xA000, and they are set in trios (p00:4248 1-2-3, p00:424E 4-5-6, p00:426A 7-8-9, p00:4270 10-11-12, p00:4276 13-14-15), with their copy at 0xF0F1-0xF0F3.

The code in banks 6, 9, 12 and 15 runs at 0xA000 with 1 and 2 below: these are the zone banks (p00:4913-4925), noted at 0xF0F6. To call a routine that switches banks and come back with their own in place they use the trampolines at p00:4050-4195: each one calls the routine and ends at p00:4198, which sets 1-2-3 and puts the bank in 0xF0F6 back at 0xA000.

The interrupt

p00:41AC hangs from H.TIMI. Every frame it sets banks 4 and 5 and calls the sound driver (bank 4, 0x6000); then, if the previous frame has finished (0xC005), it calls the frame's game (p01:6672).

Sound

Four channels of 0x20 bytes from 0xC010 over the PSG (p04:6000). There are 95 sounds (index p04:6892): below 0x29 they are effects on the fourth channel, from 0x29 to 0x5B three-channel music and from 0x5C on four-channel (p04:67FD). The lists have two formats and five instruments of 12 notes (p04:652B).

Sprites

V9938 sprite mode 2, 16 × 16. There are two attribute tables (0xF600 and 0xF200) swapped every frame with R#5 (p15:B8BC), and the order in which the sprite groups are written changes every frame (p15:B8DE), so that with more than eight on a line it is not always the same one that disappears.
